## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added

- Initial Copilot key provider for unpackaged Win32 executables.
- Cold launch and best-effort warm window activation.
- Local MSIX build, ephemeral signing key, machine trust ownership ledger, and cleanup.
- English and Simplified Chinese documentation.
- Static and integration tests.
